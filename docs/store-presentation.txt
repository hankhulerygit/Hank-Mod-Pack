# 🎿 Hank Mod Pack
**Four mods in one DLL for Jibbers Playtest** (needs JibbersModLoader). Each mod has its own entry in **Options > Mods**, so you can turn them on and off separately.

## 🧍 Improved Spawn
Join friends in one click.
• Press **F6 / Insert** (or a gamepad button) to open the list of online players
• Spawn next to them: forward/back, right/left, height (defaults -2 / 1.5 / 0.1)
• **Copy their speed and angle** to arrive moving like them
• **Spectator camera** follows the highlighted player, and a **free spectator view** lets you orbit (keyboard or gamepad: sticks, R1/L1 distance, L2/R2 field of view)
• The game's controls are muted while the panel is open
• English / Français

## 🎬 Improved Replay Editor
Keyframes for effects, right in the replay's keyframe panel, with a **second timeline** under the game's.
• **Time of day** with **sun direction, sun height and moon phase**
• **Camera shake**: slow, natural hand-held motion (strength, speed, smoothness, rotation / position / roll)
• **Image effects**: Lens & Focus, Exposure & Tone, Color, Stylize (the sliders of the Camera Effects menu)
• Every tab has **Add Keyframe, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete, Reset values**, and a coloured dot that matches its icon on the timeline
• Values blend between keyframes, outside them the game gets its own values back
• The timeline follows trimmed clips
• **Save / Load / Delete**: save game keyframes, effect keyframes or both under a name, in a folder per map. Presets fit longer or shorter replays. **F11** quick save, **F10** deletes the keyframe under the playhead

## 🏷️ Overlay
Trick banners after a clean landing, in the game's own font, with sounds that grow with the trick. Size, position, short/full names, volume, ignore grabs. **F7** opens the media folder, **F8** tests.

## 🎉 Fun
Up to **5 events**: land a trick, get a **GIF or video + a sound**. Pick the trick for each event in the menu and drop the files in `Fun Overlay\1` to `5`.
Ready to use:
1️⃣ Backflip → Rick Astley
2️⃣ Rotation of 1620 or more → Helicopter
3️⃣ Any triple → "OH BABY A TRIPLE"
4️⃣ Misty and 5️⃣ Bio → add your own files

## 📦 Install
1. Remove old `Improved Spawn.dll`, `IRE.dll`, `Overlay.dll` from `JibbersMods\cs`
2. Put `Hank Mod Pack.dll` in `JibbersMods\cs`
3. Restart the game
Everything lives in `JibbersMods\Hank Mod Pack\`.

## ⚠️ Good to know
• Replay editor effects use the game's Camera Effects menu: opening it pauses the mod's effects for a moment
• Mods that react to the time of day (night lighting...) will react to time-of-day keyframes
• Found a bug? Send the `jibbers_hotload.log` file.
