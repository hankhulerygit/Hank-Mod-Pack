Hank Mod Pack is a set of four mods for Jibbers Playtest, built into a single DLL for JibbersModLoader. Each mod has its own entry and its own on/off switch in Options > Mods.

IMPROVED SPAWN
Pick an online player from a list and spawn next to them, with their speed and direction if you want. Set forward/back, right/left and height. A spectator camera follows the highlighted player, and a free spectator view lets you orbit with keyboard or gamepad. Works in English and French.

IMPROVED REPLAY EDITOR
Effect keyframes inside the replay's keyframe panel, shown on a second timeline under the game's own:
- Time of day, with sun direction, sun height and moon phase
- Camera shake: slow, natural, hand-held movement
- Image effects: Lens & Focus, Exposure & Tone, Color, Stylize
Every tab has Add Keyframe, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete and Reset, plus a coloured dot that matches its icon on the timeline. Values blend between keyframes. The timeline follows trimmed clips. Save, load and delete presets (game keyframes, effect keyframes or both), kept in one folder per map.

OVERLAY
Trick banners after a clean landing, in the game's own font, with sounds that grow with the trick. Choose size, position, short or full names and volume.

FUN
Up to 5 events: land a trick and a GIF or video plays with a sound. Three events come ready to use: Backflip plays Rick Astley, a rotation of 1620 or more plays the Helicopter, and any triple plays "OH BABY A TRIPLE". Add your own files for the others.

INSTALL
Put Hank Mod Pack.dll in JibbersMods\cs, remove the old separate DLLs, and restart the game. Everything else is created in JibbersMods\Hank Mod Pack.
