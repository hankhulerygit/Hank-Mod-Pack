Hank Mod Pack 1.9.10 - what's new:
- Fixed: the Cam Lamp in the replay works again (it was sometimes tied to the sun or another light by mistake).
- Fixed: the Cam Lamp stayed on after leaving the replay.
- Fixed: Lights tab, Intensity and Range of Map Lights, Head Lamp and Cam Lamp now change the lights in the replay.
- Fixed: keyframe presets are now kept per map even when the game gives no map name (the map is recognised by its terrain and named Map-xxxxxx).
- Fixed: saved keyframes no longer load on other maps; each map has its own presets, and their default name starts with the map name.
- New: Rename keyframes in the keyframe panel.
- Fixed: loading saved keyframes now brings back all the replay's camera keyframes, not only the first one (the game adds the missing ones itself).
- Effects window (R2): Up on the first tab jumps to the last tab (Keyframes settings).
- The update message on the game menu now resizes to the window font.
- New: window fonts. Pick the font of the pack's windows (quick menu, effects window, HUD, messages) among 48 fonts: the game's own, the Mod Tool's and Windows'. They come with the mod. Themes window: L2 / R2 change the font (L1 / R1 the theme); also in Options > Mods > HUD > Window font.
- Fixed: the replay editor, Follow cam and POV disappearing after opening another replay.
- Fixed: Follow cam and POV settings (orbit, reactivity, lead-ahead, shake, smoothing, stabilizer, eye offsets...) now blend between keyframes; the effects window edits the keyframe at the playhead.
- Effects window (R2): the keyframe presets tab is now called "Keyframes settings" and is the last tab.
